A personal history

Literacy Journey

The forms change. The work of making meaning continues.

  1. 1986

    Early Reader

    Advanced reader placed into reading groups in first grade. Frustrated by reading materials that felt too easy.

  2. 1990s

    Voracious Reader

    Sweet Valley Twins, The Baby-Sitters Club, and stacks of books consumed at an alarming rate.

  3. Early Digital

    Digital Participant

    AOL chat rooms, AIM away messages, and MySpace introduced a new kind of literacy built around social signals and unwritten digital rules.

  4. Social Media

    Algorithmic Reader

    Increasing awareness that algorithms shape what information we encounter, reinforcing interests and sometimes creating echo chambers.

  5. Now

    Multimodal Learner

    Questioning whether literacy should include visual storytelling, video, graphic novels, sound, design, and other modes of communication.
