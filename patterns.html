Over time

From a literacy baseline to visual meaning-making.

Module 01 establishes a primarily print and digital baseline while beginning to question what counts as literacy.

Module 02 develops stronger multimodal and visual awareness through attention to audience, purpose, platform, design, and the ways different modes work together.

Module 03 makes visual rhetoric more explicit by examining how images, image-text relationships, sequence, layout, and closure create meaning and reshape the reader’s understanding.

These comparisons are subjective observations, not evaluations or scores.
